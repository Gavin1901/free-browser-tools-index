# Last Frost Date for Zone 8a: How to Plan Planting

USDA hardiness zone and local frost date answer different questions. Zone 8a describes typical minimum winter temperature, while the last spring frost depends on local weather history and microclimate.

Use the [planting calendar](https://plantingcalendar.net/) to build a crop schedule around your location and frost window. Tender crops should be timed from the local last-frost estimate, while cool-season crops may be started earlier.

Check a nearby weather station before transplanting because a regional estimate is not a guarantee.

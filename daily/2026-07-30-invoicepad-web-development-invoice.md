# Web Development Invoice Template: What Clients Expect in 2026

Web development invoices fail when the client cannot connect the total to the work delivered.

A clear invoice should separate:

- discovery and planning;
- front-end development;
- back-end or API integration;
- testing and bug fixes;
- deployment;
- maintenance or support;
- taxes, discounts, and payment terms.

The invoice should also include a unique invoice number, issue date, due date, client details, accepted payment method, and a short description for every line item.

InvoicePad provides a free browser-based [web development invoice template](https://invoicepad.net/web-developer-invoice-template) that generates a clean PDF without an account or watermark. Invoice data stays in the browser.

Use the template as a starting point, then adjust the line items to match the actual statement of work.

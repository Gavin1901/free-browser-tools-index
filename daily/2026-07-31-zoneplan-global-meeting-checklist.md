# Global Meeting Scheduling Checklist for Distributed Teams

Scheduling across time zones is not just a conversion problem. A technically valid hour can still be unfair to one part of the team.

List every participant's location, compare local working hours, account for daylight-saving changes, rotate inconvenient recurring times, include the time zone in invitations, and provide an asynchronous alternative.

The [ZonePlan global meeting planner](https://zoneplan.net/global-meeting-planner/) helps compare several locations and identify overlapping working hours.

Before sending the invitation, verify the actual date and final local time for every critical participant.

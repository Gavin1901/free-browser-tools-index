# TDEE Basics: Estimating Daily Energy Needs Without False Precision

Total Daily Energy Expenditure estimates the calories a person uses in a day. It combines resting needs with movement, exercise, and digestion.

A calculator is a starting point, not a metabolic measurement. Activity multipliers are broad, and similar people can have different real-world needs.

Use a [free TDEE calculator](https://freetdee.com/) for an initial estimate, then compare it with consistent weight and intake trends over several weeks.

Avoid reacting to one day of data. Adjust gradually and seek qualified guidance when health conditions or medication affect energy needs.

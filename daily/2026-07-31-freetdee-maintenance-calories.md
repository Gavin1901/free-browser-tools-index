# Maintenance Calories: A Practical Way to Test Your Estimate

Maintenance calories are the approximate daily intake that keeps body weight stable over time. A formula provides a baseline, but the estimate should be tested against real data.

Start with a [TDEE and maintenance calorie calculator](https://freetdee.com/). Track intake and morning weight consistently for two to three weeks and compare weekly averages.

If the average trend is stable, the estimate is close. If it moves consistently, adjust gradually. Maintenance is better understood as a range than a perfect fixed number.

# Why WhatsApp Freezes an iPhone Live Photo

An Apple Live Photo is a package containing a still image and a short motion clip. When it leaves the Apple ecosystem, WhatsApp may deliver only the still frame, especially to Android or desktop recipients.

The practical fix is to turn the motion into a standard GIF before sharing. A GIF keeps the visible movement in one file and works across iPhone, Android, Windows, and WhatsApp Web.

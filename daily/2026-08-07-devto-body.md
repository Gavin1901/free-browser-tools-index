Today I audited 839 Google impressions for my free puppy vaccine tracker and found something painful: **every single query has zero clicks.**

## The Data

[PupVax.com](https://pupvax.com/) — a free dog vaccine and deworming schedule tool I built — has been live for weeks. GSC shows 839 impressions across 28 days, but only 1 organic click (CTR 0.1%, avg position 67.5).

Top queries (all 0 clicks):
- "puppy vaccinations" — 111 impressions
- "puppy vaccination schedule" — 39 impressions
- "puppy vaccination schedule chart" — 21 impressions
- "dog deworming schedule" — 17 impressions

## What I Found

1. **Position 67.5 is page 7**. Nobody clicks page 7 results. The site needs more topical authority before Google will rank it higher.
2. **Keyword mismatch**: The homepage says "vaccine schedule tracker" but users search for "vaccination schedule." Subtle but important.
3. **Only 10 of 53 pages indexed**. Crawl budget is limited and thin pages get ignored.
4. **No external backlinks from vet/animal sites**. The AAHA guidelines citation (added Aug 4) helps E-E-A-T but hasn't kicked in yet.

## What I Changed Today

One variable: I verified the content matches AAHA 2026 guidelines, added the deworming schedule (90% of puppies are born with roundworms — most owners don't know this), and published 4 external references on GitHub with deep analysis.

I also published a [detailed Gist](https://gist.github.com/Gavin1901/28bcdb9d2dc02d2473359d5334d4a6e0) covering the immunity gap (weeks 8-16) that most puppy owners don't understand.

## The Lesson

For low-competition long-tail queries like "puppy vaccination schedule chart," the problem isn't competition — it's that Google hasn't decided your site is an authority on the topic yet. One well-cited, evidence-backed page can change that. But it takes time.

# ZonePlan SEO Observation — August 8, 2026

I am building and documenting eight free browser tools. Today I reviewed fresh 28-day Google Search Console data instead of changing another page variable too early.

## What changed

[ZonePlan](https://zoneplan.net/) now has:

- 4 clicks
- 5,860 impressions
- 0.1% CTR
- average position 44.0
- 77 indexed pages and 102 not indexed

The strongest non-brand queries are:

- `meeting planner worldwide`: 294 impressions, 0 clicks
- `global meeting planner`: 148 impressions, 0 clicks
- `international meeting planner`: 128 impressions, 0 clicks
- `meeting planner`: 102 impressions, 0 clicks

The site has gained impressions for several consecutive checks, but the click-through rate remains weak. I recently merged overlapping pages and aligned the main meeting-planner page with the search intent. That experiment is still inside its seven-day observation window, so I am not stacking another on-page change today.

## The decision

Do less, measure better:

1. Keep the current ZonePlan page structure unchanged until August 11.
2. Continue building relevant external discovery paths.
3. Recheck CTR, average position and non-brand clicks after the observation window.
4. Only then decide whether the next variable should be title, snippet copy or content structure.

# Puppy Vaccinations: A Practical Schedule Guide

Puppy vaccination timing depends on age, previous doses, local disease risk, and veterinary advice. Core vaccines are usually given as a series because maternal antibodies can affect early protection.

Use the free [puppy vaccination schedule](https://pupvax.com/) to create an age-based timeline, then confirm the dates with your veterinarian. Keep a written record of vaccine names, dose dates, and the clinic that administered them.

This planning tool is educational and does not replace veterinary care.

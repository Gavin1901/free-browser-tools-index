# What Is a LIVP File and How Do You Open It?

A LIVP file is commonly associated with an exported Apple Live Photo package. It may contain the still image and motion component needed to preserve the Live Photo experience outside the Photos app.

Use [LivePhotoKit](https://livephotokit.com/) to inspect or convert Live Photo and HEIC-related files in the browser. The tool is useful when you need a regular image, a motion clip, or a shareable format.

Keep the original file until you confirm that the converted result includes the image quality and motion you need.

# How to Schedule a Meeting Across Time Zones Without Guessing

A reliable workflow starts with named locations, a specific date, and acceptable local-hour boundaries. Do not rely on memorized offsets because daylight-saving rules change.

Collect each participant's location, set a reasonable local window, compare the overlap on the actual meeting date, and rotate the burden when no time is comfortable for everyone.

This guide to [scheduling meetings across time zones](https://zoneplan.net/blog/schedule-meeting-across-time-zones/) explains the process and links to a planner.

For recurring meetings, recheck the schedule before seasonal clock changes.

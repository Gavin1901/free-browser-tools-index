# Puppy Deworming Timeline: Questions to Ask Your Veterinarian

Deworming plans vary with age, weight, exposure, test results, geography, and parasite risk. Ask which parasites are common locally, whether testing is recommended, what dose is appropriate, and when treatment should repeat.

The [dog deworming schedule chart](https://pupvax.com/dog-deworming-schedule-chart/) provides an educational timeline and helps owners keep records between visits.

Never guess a dose or use medication intended for another species. Persistent symptoms or a sick young puppy require veterinary assessment.

# Freelancer Invoice Checklist: What to Include Before Sending

A professional invoice should make it easy for a client to understand what they bought, what they owe, and how to pay. Check the business and client details, unique invoice number, issue and due dates, clear line items, rates, tax, currency, total, and payment instructions.

Avoid vague descriptions such as "services." A line like "Landing page development - final delivery" gives both sides a cleaner record.

[InvoicePad's free invoice generator](https://invoicepad.net/) helps freelancers prepare and export a PDF invoice in the browser without creating an account.

Before sending, open the exported PDF and verify the client name, invoice number, dates, currency, total, and payment details.

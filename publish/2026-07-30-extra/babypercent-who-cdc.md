# WHO vs CDC Growth Charts

WHO and CDC growth references are related but not interchangeable.

- WHO standards are commonly used for younger children and describe growth under specified healthy conditions.
- CDC references describe the distribution observed in a US population and are commonly used for older children.
- Age, sex, measurement method, and chart selection all affect the reported percentile.

The calculators at [BabyPercent](https://babypercent.com/growth-percentile-calculator/) help visualize weight, length/height, and head-circumference percentiles.

Always use the chart recommended by the child’s clinician and focus on longitudinal trends rather than reacting to a single percentile.

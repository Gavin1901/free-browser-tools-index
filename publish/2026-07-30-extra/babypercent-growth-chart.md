# How to Read a Baby Growth Percentile

A growth percentile compares a child’s measurement with a reference population of children of the same age and sex. It is not a school grade and a higher number is not automatically better.

The most useful signal is usually the pattern over time:

- repeated measurements taken accurately;
- a reasonably consistent growth trajectory;
- interpretation alongside feeding, development, health history, and clinical context.

[BabyPercent](https://babypercent.com/) provides WHO/CDC-style percentile calculations and charts for informational use.

One measurement cannot diagnose a growth problem. Parents should discuss unexpected changes, feeding concerns, or developmental symptoms with a qualified pediatric professional.

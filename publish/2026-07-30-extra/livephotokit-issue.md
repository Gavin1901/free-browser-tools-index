## Public use-case note

LivePhotoKit now has focused browser-based routes for common Apple photo compatibility problems:

- [HEIC to JPG](https://livephotokit.com/heic-to-jpg/)
- [Open LIVP files](https://livephotokit.com/open-livp-file/)
- [LIVP to MP4](https://livephotokit.com/livp-to-mp4/)
- [Live Photo to GIF](https://livephotokit.com/live-photo-to-gif/)

The clearest product promise is fast conversion without account creation. This issue records the public discovery routes used in the current indexing and usability review.

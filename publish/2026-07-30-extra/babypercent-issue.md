## Public product note

[BabyPercent](https://babypercent.com/) provides informational WHO/CDC-style growth percentile calculators and charts.

The product is designed to explain:

- weight-for-age;
- length/height-for-age;
- head circumference;
- the difference between one measurement and a longitudinal trend.

It does not diagnose medical conditions. Unexpected growth changes or feeding and development concerns should be reviewed with a qualified pediatric professional.

## Public compatibility note

[iWorkViewer](https://iworkviewer.com/) helps Windows users understand practical options for Apple iWork files:

- Pages to DOCX or PDF;
- Numbers to XLSX or PDF;
- Keynote to PPTX or PDF;
- safe handling of unfamiliar `.pages`, `.numbers`, and `.key` files.

This issue records the public project route used in the current compatibility and search-discovery review.

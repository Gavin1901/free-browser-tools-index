## Public discovery note

[Planting Calendar](https://plantingcalendar.net/) provides location-aware planting guidance built around frost timing, crop type, and practical seed-starting windows.

Key user intents covered:

- local planting calendar;
- last spring and first fall frost;
- indoor seed-starting dates;
- transplant timing;
- vegetable-specific planting guidance.

This issue records the public product route used for the current search-discovery review. Frost dates remain estimates and should be checked against local forecasts.

# Puppy Worming Timeline: Four Mistakes New Owners Make

Puppies can encounter intestinal parasites before birth, while nursing, and through contaminated soil or stool. The hard part is not remembering the word “deworming”; it is knowing when the plan changes.

Four common mistakes:

1. Waiting until worms are visible.
2. Treating one negative stool test as permanent proof.
3. Assuming intestinal-worm treatment and heartworm prevention are identical.
4. Reusing a dose after the puppy’s weight has changed.

A good timeline helps owners record dates and prepare questions, while the veterinarian selects the product and dose.

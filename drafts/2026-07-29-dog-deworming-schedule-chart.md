# Dog Deworming Schedule Chart: What Changes From Puppy to Adult

A useful dog deworming schedule is not one fixed dose repeated forever. The timing changes as a puppy grows, starts monthly parasite prevention, and reaches adulthood.

## Practical age checkpoints

| Age | What to discuss with your veterinarian |
|---|---|
| About 2 weeks | Starting a veterinarian-selected deworming product |
| 4, 6 and 8 weeks | Repeating treatment at the interval on the veterinary plan |
| 2 to 6 months | Moving toward monthly broad-spectrum parasite control |
| Adult dogs | Year-round prevention and fecal testing based on exposure risk |

Medication choice and dosage depend on weight, health, location and the parasites being targeted. A schedule is a preparation tool, not a prescription.

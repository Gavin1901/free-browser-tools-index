# How to Choose a Fair Global Meeting Time

The best international meeting time is not simply the first hour that appears open on everyone’s calendar.

## A practical process

1. Add every participant by city rather than using ambiguous abbreviations such as CST or IST.
2. Compare local working hours after daylight-saving adjustments.
3. Avoid forcing the same office into early mornings or late nights every week.
4. Rotate the inconvenience for recurring meetings.
5. Record the agreed time in UTC as well as local time.
6. Recheck the schedule when daylight-saving rules change.

## Example

A New York, London, and Bangalore team may have only a narrow overlap. If there is no comfortable hour for all three locations, choose the least disruptive window and rotate it at the next meeting rather than permanently penalizing one office.

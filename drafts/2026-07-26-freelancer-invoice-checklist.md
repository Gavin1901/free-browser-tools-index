# Freelancer Invoice Checklist for Faster Client Approval

A freelance invoice is easier to approve when the client can understand it without opening the original project brief.

## Before sending

1. Use a unique invoice number.
2. Add the exact issue date and due date.
3. Use the client’s legal business name.
4. List deliverables, milestones, hours, or retainers separately.
5. Show deposits, discounts, tax, and the remaining balance.
6. Include the agreed payment method and currency.
7. Add a purchase order number when the client requires one.
8. Send the PDF to both your project contact and accounts-payable address.

## Better line-item wording

Avoid vague descriptions such as “freelance work.”

Use descriptions such as:

- Landing page design and development, milestone 2 of 2
- SEO consulting, May 1–15, 12 hours at $100 per hour
- Monthly content retainer, June 2026

Specific descriptions reduce clarification emails and help finance teams approve payment faster.

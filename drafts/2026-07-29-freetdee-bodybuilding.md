# TDEE for Bodybuilding: Bulk, Cut or Maintain

For bodybuilding, TDEE is the baseline—not the final target. Maintenance starts near estimated TDEE, a cut uses a modest deficit, and a gaining phase uses a controlled surplus.

Track weekly weight trends and gym performance instead of reacting to one day of scale noise. Recalculate when body weight or activity changes materially.

The free [TDEE calculator for bodybuilding](https://freetdee.com/) provides a transparent starting estimate plus BMR and macro context.

# Maintenance Calories: A Practical TDEE Starting Point

Maintenance calories are the estimated daily calories that keep body weight broadly stable. They are not a fixed biological constant.

Start with a TDEE estimate, keep food tracking and activity reasonably consistent for two to three weeks, then compare the predicted result with the real weight trend.

Use the free [FreeTDEE calculator](https://freetdee.com/) to estimate maintenance calories, BMR and an initial macro range. Change one variable at a time when adjusting the estimate.

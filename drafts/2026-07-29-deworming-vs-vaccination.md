# Deworming vs Vaccination: Why Puppies Need Two Different Timelines

Vaccines train the immune system against specific diseases. Deworming and parasite prevention target organisms such as roundworms, hookworms or other parasites. One calendar cannot safely substitute for the other.

When organizing a first-year puppy health plan, keep separate records for:

- vaccine names and booster dates;
- deworming or parasite-control products;
- the puppy’s current weight;
- fecal-test results;
- adverse reactions or symptoms;
- the next veterinary appointment.

Some monthly products cover several parasites, but coverage differs by label and region. Owners should confirm exactly what a product covers rather than assuming “monthly prevention” means everything.

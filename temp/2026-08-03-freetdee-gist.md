# Bodybuilding TDEE: How to Set Calories for Cutting and Bulking Without Guessing

"Eat 500 below maintenance to cut" is common advice. But if you do not know your actual maintenance number, you are guessing.

## TDEE for Bodybuilders: The Numbers That Matter

Your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) depends on four things:
1. **BMR** — calories your body burns at complete rest.
2. **NEAT** — non-exercise activity (walking, standing, fidgeting).
3. **EAT** — planned exercise and training sessions.
4. **TEF** — the thermic effect of digesting food (about 10% of intake).

For bodybuilders, the activity multiplier is where most calculators go wrong. A "very active" desk worker is not the same as a "very active" lifter training 5x per week.

## A Practical Starting Point

- **Cutting**: TDEE minus 300-500 kcal. Do not drop below BMR.
- **Lean bulk**: TDEE plus 200-300 kcal. More is not faster.
- **Maintenance**: Eat at TDEE for 2 weeks and watch the scale before adjusting.

# Puppy Vaccinations: A Complete Schedule From 6 Weeks to 16 Weeks

Bringing home a puppy means taking on a vaccination schedule that starts at 6 weeks and runs through 16 weeks — and if you miss a window, you may need to restart the series.

## The Core Puppy Vaccine Timeline

| Age | Core Vaccines |
|---|---|
| 6-8 weeks | DHPP (Distemper, Hepatitis, Parainfluenza, Parvo) |
| 10-12 weeks | DHPP booster |
| 14-16 weeks | DHPP final + Rabies |
| 12-16 weeks | Bordetella (kennel cough), optional |

## Why the Schedule Is Strict

Puppies receive maternal antibodies through their mother's milk. These antibodies protect them early on but also interfere with vaccines. The standard 3-dose DHPP series is timed to catch the window when maternal antibodies fade but before the puppy is exposed to real pathogens.

Missing a booster by more than 2-4 weeks may require restarting the series. This is not a "close enough" situation.

## Deworming Runs in Parallel

Most puppies carry roundworms from birth. Deworming usually starts at 2 weeks (with the breeder) and continues at 4, 6, and 8 weeks during vet visits.

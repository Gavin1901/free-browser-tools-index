# Freelance Writer Invoice Template: What to Include in 2026

If you write for a living, your invoice is the last thing a client sees before they pay you. A clear invoice gets paid faster. A vague one sits in someone's inbox.

## The 7 Fields Every Freelance Writer Invoice Needs

1. **Your business name and contact** — even if it is just your name and email.
2. **Client name and billing contact** — the person who approves payment, not just your editor.
3. **Invoice number and date** — sequential numbering saves you during tax season.
4. **Clear service descriptions** — "Blog post (1,200 words): 5 Ways to Reduce SaaS Churn" beats "Writing — $300".
5. **Rate and total** — per-word, per-project, or per-hour. Be explicit.
6. **Payment terms** — Net 15, Net 30, or due on receipt. Spell it out.
7. **Payment method** — PayPal link, bank details, or whatever you actually use.

## The One Field Most Writers Forget

**Revision policy.** If you include one round of revisions in your rate, say so. If extra rounds cost extra, write it down before the project starts. The invoice itself can be a reminder: "Includes 1 round of revisions. Additional revisions billed at $75/hour."

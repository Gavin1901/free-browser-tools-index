# Puppy Vaccination Schedule: What the 2022 AAHA Guidelines Actually Say

If you just got a puppy, the vaccination schedule can look like alphabet soup: DHPP, DAPP, DA2PP, bordetella, lepto, rabies. Different vets use different brand names. Online forums give conflicting advice.

Here is what the authoritative sources actually say.

## Core vaccines (every puppy needs these)

Per the **AAHA Canine Vaccination Guidelines (2022)** and **AVMA Vaccination Principles**:

| Age | Vaccine | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 6-8 weeks | DHPP #1 | Distemper, Hepatitis, Parainfluenza, Parvovirus |
| 10-12 weeks | DHPP #2 | Booster |
| 14-16 weeks | DHPP #3 | Final puppy booster — this is the critical one |
| 12-16 weeks | Rabies | Required by law; timing varies by state |

**Why the 14-16 week booster matters**: Maternal antibodies from the mother's milk can interfere with vaccines given before 14 weeks. The AAHA specifically recommends the final DHPP booster at or after 16 weeks to ensure immunity takes hold.

## Non-core vaccines (depends on lifestyle)

- **Bordetella** (kennel cough): Recommended if your puppy will be boarded, attend daycare, or visit dog parks
- **Leptospirosis**: Recommended in areas with standing water, wildlife, or farms
- **Lyme**: Recommended in tick-endemic regions (Northeast, Upper Midwest, Pacific Coast)
- **Canine Influenza**: Recommended for social dogs in outbreak areas

## Deworming schedule

Per the **CDC** and **AAHA**, puppies should be dewormed:

- 2, 4, 6, 8 weeks of age (usually done by breeder or shelter)
- Then monthly until 6 months
- Then based on fecal exam results and lifestyle risk

## The free, browser-based solution

[iWorkViewer.com](https://iworkviewer.com/) is a free online tool that opens Apple iWork files — Pages (.pages), Numbers (.numbers), and Keynote (.key) — directly in your browser. No app install. No Mac required. No sign-up.

### How to use it on Android

1. **Save the attachment** — In Gmail, tap the `.pages` attachment and choose "Save to Drive" or "Download"
2. **Open iWorkViewer** — Go to [iworkviewer.com](https://iworkviewer.com/) in Chrome or any mobile browser
3. **Tap "Choose File"** — Select the `.pages` file from your Downloads or Drive
4. **Read immediately** — The document renders in your browser as formatted text

### What it handles

- `.pages` files from Mac, iPad, and iCloud
- `.numbers` spreadsheets
- `.key` Keynote presentations
- All three formats render in-browser without conversion

### Android-specific tips

- **Samsung Internet users**: The file picker works the same as Chrome. Navigate to "My Files" → "Downloads"
- **Xiaomi/Redmi users**: Use the "Files" app to locate the attachment, then "Open with" → Chrome → navigate to iWorkViewer
- **Pixel users**: Files downloaded from Gmail go to the "Downloads" folder accessible from the Files app

### Privacy

Files are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Your documents stay on your device.
